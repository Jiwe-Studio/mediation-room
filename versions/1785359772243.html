The Mediation Room

A prototype from Donkosira × Jiwe Games for Thought × SOAS × Code for Africa

This is a prototype. All testimony you'll read here is synthetic — written for this demo, not a real person's words. It exists to help you understand how testimony changes as it becomes public, not to let you "talk to a survivor."

You are not here to talk to a victim. You are here to understand what can be preserved, what must be protected, and what may be lost when testimony becomes public.

This prototype uses synthetic testimony created for demonstration purposes only. It is not a simulation of a real survivor, a replacement for testimony or human facilitation, or an oracle of historical truth. Its purpose is to test the interaction model, ethical boundaries, refusal rules, and mediation labels before any real testimony is ever involved. Built by Jiwe Games for Thought with Donkosira, SOAS (Marie Rodet), and Code for Africa's AI Sandbox fellowship.
