# Methodology Note

**The Mediation Room** is a seed prototype from Donkosira, Jiwe Games for
Thought, SOAS (Marie Rodet), and Code for Africa's AI Sandbox fellowship.

All testimony presented in this prototype is **synthetic** — written
specifically to test this experience, not drawn from a real witness. This
prototype:

- is **not** a simulation of a real survivor,
- is **not** a replacement for testimony, historical research, or human
  facilitation,
- is **not** an oracle of historical truth.

It exists to test an interaction model: whether AI can help preserve key
lived knowledge from anonymised testimony while making the limits, risks,
and distortions of mediation visible to the person interacting with it.

A testimony is never transferred unchanged from person to public. It passes
through layers — the original lived experience, the person's memory and
narration of it, research mediation (interviewing, transcription,
translation, anonymisation, summarisation, interpretation), and AI mediation
(retrieval, prompting, synthesis, refusal rules, interface design). Each
layer can protect meaning. Each layer can also change it. This prototype
asks users to engage with that process, not just with a finished answer.

Every response in this prototype carries a mediation label explaining what
kind of answer it is — synthetic demo testimony, context-supported
interpretation, a synthesised pattern, something withheld to protect
identity, or something the available material simply doesn't support. The
system also refuses categories of question on purpose: names, exact
locations, family lines, raw testimony, speculative accusation, and
requests to roleplay as a real victim are all out of scope by design, not
by accident. Those refusals are part of the experience, not a failure of
it — they demonstrate why sensitive testimony has to be mediated carefully
in the first place.

Future implementations of this project may use real testimony, provided by
Donkosira, reviewed and approved for public use. This prototype exists to
test the ethical boundaries, refusal rules, and interface before that ever
happens.

Questions or concerns about this prototype can be directed to the project
team via Donkosira or Jiwe Games for Thought.
